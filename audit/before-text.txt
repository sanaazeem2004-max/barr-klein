UNCOMPROMISING ADVOCATES

With a combined experience of over thirty years, Benjamin Barr and Stephen Klein are uncompromising advocates in state and federal courts, in trials and appeals, and before regulatory bodies. Especially known for our experience with constitutional law, we advise clients on a host of issues including business formation, nonprofit compliance, campaign finance law, privacy law, the First Amendment, and journalism practices.

In every instance it is our goal to advance your goals and your voice—not the preferences of bureaucrats.

Our practice is eclectic. Benjamin Barr served as a counsel to two Chairmen of the Federal Election Commission and has fought for free speech rights across the country. Stephen Klein has helped reform civil asset forfeiture and has changed election law nationwide.

We are united by the principle that individual rights—your rights—come first.

If you are being censored, whether by the government’s heavy hand or clever red tape, or being sued by someone who’s just trying to shut you up, please be in touch.

Benjamin Barr is licensed to practice law in Illinois and is also admitted to practice before the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit. Benjamin Barr is not admitted to the District of Columbia Bar, and his practice in Washington, D.C. is limited to federal court matters in accordance with D.C. Court of Appeals Rule 49. Stephen Klein is licensed in the District of Columbia, Michigan and Illinois.
